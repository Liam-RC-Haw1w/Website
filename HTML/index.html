Our Mission

// Mission is mentioned at the wpdeafcricket.co.za website

"Building a strong deaf cricket community through support and opportunity. "Our mission is to provide a nurturing and competitive environment for deaf cricketers, equipping them with the skills, resources, and opportunities needed to excel. We are committed to raising awareness, promoting inclusivity, and building a supportive network that champions the growth of deaf cricket in South Africa and beyond.
