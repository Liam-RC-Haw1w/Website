Our History

Western Province Deaf Cricket was established to fill a crucial gap in South African sports, providing opportunities for deaf and hard-of-hearing cricket players to participate in competitive sport at regional and national levels.

Over the past 30 years, we have grown from a small group of passionate players into a well-organized cricket club that represents the Western Province region. We have developed our skills, won championships, and most importantly, created a supportive community where athletes can thrive.

Our organisation plays a key role in advancing inclusivity in sport, breaking down barriers, and demonstrating that cricket is for everyone, regardless of hearing ability.
